# Weekwell

A calm, simple weekly timetable for classes, study sessions, and the rest of your week. Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, so there is nothing to install.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in a browser. Your schedule is saved in that browser with local storage. Add a class, click any existing plan to edit or remove it, move between weeks, or export your timetable as a CSV file.

## Customize

The starter schedule lives in `app.js` as `sampleEvents`. The first time the app runs, it loads those sample classes. Changes are stored locally in your browser.

## Privacy

Weekwell has no backend. Your timetable stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.
